Privacy

DotEnvUp does not collect, store, or process your secrets or any personal data.

Zero-knowledge, no server

DotEnvUp is a local-first tool. Encryption and decryption happen on your machine. Your private keys stay in ~/.dotenvup/identity. We do not operate a server that receives your secrets, keys, or .env files. There is no account, no signup, and no cloud storage for the open-source DotEnvUp extension and CLI.

Extension and CLI

The VS Code/Cursor extension and the up CLI run entirely on your device. They do not send your .env, .env.up, or key material to us or any third party. No telemetry or analytics are collected by the open-source DotEnvUp project.

This website

dotenvup.com is a static site (e.g. GitHub Pages). We do not use cookies or track you for the purpose of the DotEnvUp product. If the site is hosted by a provider that collects access logs or similar data, that is governed by the host’s privacy policy.

Team sharing (UnknownPassword)

If you use UnknownPassword or other services built on the .env.up format, their privacy practices are separate. Check their respective privacy policies.

Contact

For privacy-related questions about DotEnvUp, open an issue or discussion on GitHub, or contact the maintainers through the repository.
